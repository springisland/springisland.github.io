SI Trails Privacy Policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

SI Trails is a trail map app for Spring Island, South Carolina. This page explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your location never leaves your phone, and we do not know who you are.

Your location

The app uses your phone's location to show where you are on the map, to give directions, and to record a track when you ask it to. While you are recording a track or following a route, the app keeps using your location with the screen off so the recording continues.

All of this happens on your phone. Your location is not sent to us or to anyone else.

Your tracks, routes and waypoints

Tracks you record, routes you save and waypoints you name are stored only on your phone. They are removed when you delete them or delete the app.

If you choose to share a route or waypoint, the app hands it to the sharing option you pick (for example Messages or Mail). It goes only to the people you send it to.

Anonymous usage statistics

To learn which features are useful, the app sends anonymous usage statistics through a service called TelemetryDeck. These record things like "a route was planned" or "a track was saved," along with general details such as the app version, the type of phone, and rounded figures such as the length of a route.

These statistics never include your location, your name, your contacts, or the names you give to tracks, routes and waypoints. They are not linked to your identity, are not used for advertising, and are not used to track you across other apps or websites.

What the app does not do

  • It has no accounts and asks for no personal details.
  • It shows no advertising.
  • It does not sell or share personal information.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
