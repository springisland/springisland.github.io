SI Trails

The trail map app for Spring Island, South Carolina

SI Trails shows where you are on the island's roads and trails, with no cell signal needed. You can plan a route, get a suggested loop, record a track, drop pins, and share routes and meeting points with others.

Getting help

In the app, tap the ? button on the map for a short tour of every tool. While the tour is open, tap any button to read about it.

Common questions

The blue dot isn't showing. Open the iPhone's Settings, find SI Trails, and set Location to "While Using the App."

Does it work without signal? Yes. The map, trails and directions are all stored in the app.

Some trails look faded. Faded trails are not open to the way you are traveling. Tap the layers button to change between foot, cart, horse and auto.

How do I share a route or a meeting point? Open the Saved list and tap the share button beside a route or waypoint.
